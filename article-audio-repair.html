What AI Audio Repair does

RIVANI AI Audio Repair is designed for spoken recordings affected by steady background noise, room tone, hum, fan or road noise, hiss-like noise floors, uneven recording level and difficult phone or laptop microphones. The goal is clearer, more comfortable speech without pretending every damaged recording can become a studio master.

The main repair path is RIVANI Clear Voice: choose or record audio, scan it, set repair strength, process it, compare Before and After, then export.

Current Beta workflow

  1. Choose audio or record from the microphone. WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG and FLAC are supported by the upload workflow.
  2. Scan the recording. RIVANI checks common quality indicators before repair.
  3. Set Clear Voice strength. Lower values preserve more ambience; stronger values remove more unwanted sound.
  4. Run the repair. Keep the tab open while the browser workflow finishes.
  5. Compare Before and After. Listen at a similar volume and judge naturalness as well as cleanliness.
  6. Export. Free Beta includes MP3 export.
Why comparison matters

Noise reduction is a trade-off. A cleaner waveform is not automatically a better voice, so RIVANI keeps Before/After listening visible.

What Free Beta includes

  • 9 successful repairs per day
  • Up to 30 minutes and 500 MB per file
  • RIVANI Clear Voice
  • Audio scan and repair-strength control
  • Microphone recording through the same workflow
  • Before/After playback
  • MP3 export

Failed or cancelled jobs should not consume a successful-job slot. Processing actions require sign-in.

Good use cases

  • Podcast and interview speech recorded outside a treated studio
  • YouTube, Shorts and Reels voiceovers
  • Lecture and classroom recordings
  • Phone, voice-note and laptop-microphone speech
  • Talking-head creator audio with steady room or fan noise

Important limits

  • Severely clipped speech may contain permanently lost information.
  • Two people talking over each other can remain difficult.
  • Strong music, heavy reverberation and rapidly changing background sound can leave artifacts.
  • Aggressive enhancement can remove breath, consonants or ambience and sound unnatural.

Privacy and processing

The selected audio is processed in the current RIVANI browser workflow. AI engine files may be downloaded to the device and cached. Keep the tab open until processing and export are complete.
