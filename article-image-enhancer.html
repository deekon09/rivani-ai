What Image Enhancer does

RIVANI Image Enhancer runs a real Real-ESRGAN x4plus super-resolution model inside the browser. The source image itself is not uploaded to a RIVANI GPU server: the model is downloaded to the device and inference runs locally.

The main product difference is Fidelity Guard. RIVANI does not treat a model output as automatically trustworthy. It downsamples and compares the enhanced result against the source, measuring sampled structure similarity, color drift and edge amplification before deciding how much AI detail to keep.

Workflow

  1. Upload, drop or paste a JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF image.
  2. Smart Scan estimates blur, noise, compression risk, lighting, fine-edge sensitivity and transparency.
  3. Choose Natural, Strong or Restore and 2× or 4× output.
  4. RIVANI runs tiled Real-ESRGAN enhancement in the browser.
  5. Fidelity Guard checks the result and automatically reduces AI strength if the output starts drifting too far from the original.
  6. Use the Before / After slider and Fidelity Report before downloading.

Where it works well

  • Low-resolution social and creator images
  • Old compressed photographs
  • Product images that need cleaner edges
  • Presentation graphics and screenshots
  • Transparent PNG graphics and logos
Fidelity Guard

RIVANI's rule is simple: enhance aggressively where the model is confident, and move back toward the original where the result becomes structurally risky. This is not a promise that missing information can be recovered perfectly.

Important limits

  • AI cannot know exact details that were never captured in the source.
  • Extremely blurred faces or unreadable text can still be reconstructed incorrectly.
  • Large 4× jobs can use significant GPU/CPU memory, so RIVANI automatically reduces the effective scale when needed for browser stability.
  • Text & Logo Safe is a conservative protection mode; it is not OCR verification.

Privacy and processing

The image stays in the browser workflow. RIVANI fetches the enhancement model from its model-delivery Worker, caches model bytes on the device where supported, and sends image pixels only to the local browser worker for inference.
