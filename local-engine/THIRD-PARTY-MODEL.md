# Third-party model notes

RIVANI Music V1 integrates with ACE-Step 1.5 as a local model runtime. The RIVANI bundle does not contain ACE-Step model weights.

Official sources:
- Project: https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5
- Model card: https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5

At the time this bundle was prepared, the upstream project/model card identified the project/model as MIT licensed and documented text-to-music, optional lyrics, 48 kHz stereo output, 10-second to 10-minute duration, reference audio, cover, repaint, track extraction/lego/complete tasks, and local consumer-hardware support. Always verify current upstream terms before public redistribution.
