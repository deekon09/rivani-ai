ABOUT RIVANI AI

Useful AI tools with a clear job to do.

RIVANI AI is being built as a collection of focused utilities rather than one general-purpose chatbot. Every tool should explain what it does, what it needs, what it returns and where its limits are.

RIVANI principlesFocused toolsTransparent statusLUKI assistantExpandable platform

Focused tools first

RIVANI AI currently focuses on three live AI media tasks — repair a voice recording, enhance an image and remove an image background — plus a free Advanced Student Calculator. Each tool gets its own interface instead of requiring the user to invent prompts.

Status and limits should be visible

Free limits, Pro-only controls, export choices and realistic failure cases should be visible before they surprise the user. RIVANI avoids presenting unfinished ideas as live tools.

LUKI is a tool guide, not a general chatbot

LUKI appears on the side of the site and answers only questions about RIVANI AI tools, features, status, pricing information published on the site, and file-handling information. If a question is unrelated, LUKI redirects the user back to the RIVANI platform.

Live tools now, roadmap clearly labeled

The live product is centered on Audio Repair, Image Enhancer, Background Remover and the Advanced Calculator. The homepage also shows a clearly labeled Upcoming Tools roadmap so future utilities are visible without pretending they are already live.

Smarter tools. Better results.
