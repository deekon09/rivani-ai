Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · This draft is written for the current RIVANI AI product direction and should be reviewed before a commercial launch.

PoliciesPrivacyTermsAcceptable UseCookies

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how RIVANI AI may handle information when you visit the website, create an account or use a tool. Individual tool screens should provide additional processing details when their behavior differs.

2. Account information

If you choose Google sign-in, Google may share basic account information such as your name, email address and profile image, depending on the permissions you approve. Email/password accounts provide the email address and username entered during signup. RIVANI AI should request only the minimum information needed for account access.

3. Files and tool inputs

Some tools may be able to process files locally in your browser while others may require a remote service. The production interface should disclose this before processing. RIVANI AI should not silently change a local-only tool into a remote-upload workflow without updating the notice.

4. Usage and technical data

The site may receive standard technical data needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and error logs. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to identify them.

5. Why information may be used

  • Provide sign-in and account functions.
  • Operate requested tools and return results.
  • Prevent abuse and protect the service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Retention

File-retention rules should be defined per tool. Temporary uploads, if any, should be deleted as soon as they are no longer required for processing unless the user explicitly chooses to save them.

7. Third-party providers

Authentication or AI-processing providers may process data under their own terms. LUKI website-assistant messages may be sent through Google Gemini API and, when needed as a fallback, GroqCloud. RIVANI AI keeps those provider API keys on the server-side Cloudflare Worker rather than exposing them in browser code. Tool-specific providers should be identified when additional integrations are enabled.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to create an account unless a tool requires it. Where applicable, you may request deletion of account data or stop using a tool before uploading a file.

9. Children and restricted use

RIVANI AI is not designed to knowingly collect information from children in ways that violate applicable law. Age requirements should be finalized before public commercial launch.

10. Changes

This policy may change as tools, authentication and processing systems are added. The “Last updated” date should be changed whenever material terms change.

11. Account deletion

A signed-in user can request account deletion from the dashboard. The request is stored server-side and enters a 7-day grace period during which it can be cancelled. After the deadline, an automated account service permanently deletes the Firebase Authentication account. Processing runs on a schedule, so deletion may complete shortly after the exact 7-day timestamp. The deletion request record is removed after successful deletion.
