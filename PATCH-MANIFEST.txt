RIVANI Turnstile cache fix

Changed files only:
- assets/runtime-config.js
- _headers

Reason:
The previous /assets/* immutable 1-year cache rule also cached assets/runtime-config.js.
That can keep the old blank Turnstile site key in browsers/CDN after deployment.

This patch:
- keeps the public Turnstile site key in assets/runtime-config.js
- marks runtime-config.js no-store/no-cache
- keeps aggressive caching only for stable media assets
- does not touch AI inference/model files
