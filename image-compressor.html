QUICK ANSWERS

Image compressor FAQ.

Can I compress an image to 50 KB or 100 KB?

Yes. Exact Size mode iterates quality and, in Strict Target mode, can reduce dimensions when needed to get under the requested target.

Does RIVANI upload my image?

The compressor decodes and re-encodes image pixels in your browser. Sign-in is used for access gating, not to upload the image to the compressor backend.

Which format should I use?

Smart mode can race supported formats. JPEG is widely compatible, WebP is efficient, PNG is useful for lossless graphics and alpha, and AVIF is tested only when the browser can encode it.

Why can an exact target reduce dimensions?

Some images cannot reach a very small byte limit at acceptable quality. Strict Target can trade resolution for bytes; Quality-first can keep more detail and report that the target was missed.
