More than a one-click background remover

RIVANI Background Remover creates a foreground alpha mask around the main person, product, graphic or object. After the automatic cutout, the user can inspect the boundary, repair mistakes, change the background and export the finished asset without rerunning AI for every small adjustment.

A transparent checkerboard is used in the preview so true transparency is visible. The downloaded transparent PNG/WebP contains real alpha transparency; the checkerboard itself is only a preview aid.

RIVANI Precision

The public interface uses one quality-first cutout path called RIVANI Precision. Users do not have to choose between a visibly worse fast mode and a better quality mode. RIVANI automatically chooses a stable execution path for the device while keeping Precision as the user-facing quality goal.

The main subject mask is only the first step. The tool also evaluates uncertain edges, allows source-guided manual correction and provides multiple backgrounds so halos become easier to notice.

Subject presets

  • Auto: general people and objects
  • Hair: portrait, hair and fur where softer alpha transitions matter
  • Product: cleaner, firmer edges for product photography
  • Glass: softer alpha behavior for translucent/transparent-looking boundaries
  • Logo: harder edges for logos, text and graphics

The preset changes edge behavior; it does not replace the original source pixels with generated subject content.

Edge precision and manual repair

  • Edge Clean increases separation around uncertain boundaries.
  • Expand / Contract keeps a little more fringe or tightens the cutout inward.
  • Feather softens the alpha transition for hair, fur and soft edges.
  • Edge Decontaminate reduces color spill from the old background around semi-transparent edge pixels.
  • Multi-subject selection can keep one detected foreground region or all regions.
  • Cutout Guard estimates edge confidence and points to the hardest alpha region for review.
  • Refine Brush supports Erase and Restore directly on the mask, plus Undo and Reset AI Mask.
Best way to inspect an edge

Check the cutout over transparency, white and black backgrounds. A halo that is hard to see on one color often becomes obvious on another.

Background replacement and finish

Once the subject is isolated, the same page can render it over Transparent, White, Black, Blur, Studio gradient, a custom solid color or a custom image. Studio Shadow can add a soft generated shadow. Product canvas presets can center the subject inside original, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 framing with adjustable padding.

Exports

  • Transparent PNG master
  • Transparent WebP for a smaller transparent file
  • Composite JPEG with the selected background applied
  • Alpha Mask PNG
  • Shadow Layer PNG
  • Asset Pack ZIP containing the cutout, alpha mask, shadow and preview

There is no watermark on the standard cutout export.

Free and Pro

Free: 9 successful background removals per local day. All standard Precision cutout quality, subject presets, edge controls, manual repair, background choices and standard exports remain available on Free. Failed jobs do not consume the daily count.

Pro: removes the daily background-removal cap. RIVANI does not reduce cutout quality for Free users to create a quality paywall. Public secure payment checkout is not connected yet, so the Pro prompt is currently an entitlement/upgrade surface rather than a completed payment flow.

Difficult images and realistic limits

  • Hair or fur against a background with nearly the same color can still require brush cleanup.
  • Transparent glass, smoke, motion blur, reflections and fine semi-transparent material are inherently difficult to separate perfectly.
  • Low-contrast shoulder/body edges can retain a small halo or lose fine detail.
  • Multiple overlapping people or objects can need manual subject selection or mask repair.
  • Changing from a light old background to a very dark new background can reveal color spill that was previously invisible.

Privacy and first-use loading

Image pixels are processed in the browser. The required AI engine is downloaded to the device and can be cached, so the first run can take longer than later runs. Large images may be adapted automatically for reliable processing on the current device.
