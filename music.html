FULL CREATION FLOW

Not a one-button black box. A controllable music studio.

Use a simple prompt or lock exact musical decisions such as duration, BPM, key, time signature, singer persona, language, structure, instruments and seed.

01

Quick Create

Describe mood, genre and story. Local planning builds the song request and ACE-Step handles stereo music generation with optional lyrics and vocals.

02

24 singer personas

Generic RIVANI vocal characters use descriptive vocal prompts plus fixed persona seeds. A reference-audio slot can guide voice/style when the user has rights and consent.

03

Regenerate without destroying

Lock singer, seed, prompt, lyrics, BPM, key or structure before creating a variation. Repaint a selected time range instead of replacing the full song.

04

Production tools

Cover/remix, selective repaint, track extraction, instrument-track generation, continuation, WAV/MP3/FLAC output, project history and local files.

05

Device-adaptive models

Turbo is default. Stronger GPUs can use larger XL profiles; lower-memory devices use CPU offload, quantization or DiT-only mode depending on the detected hardware.

06

Local-first privacy

The local engine binds to 127.0.0.1. Generated songs and uploaded references stay in the user's local workspace unless the user exports or shares them.

DEVICE REQUIREMENTS

First run is heavier than a normal website.

The main Turbo checkpoint alone is several gigabytes on disk. Low-VRAM operation may use CPU/RAM offload, so generation can be slower. CPU-only mode is available as a compatibility fallback, not a promise of fast full-song generation.

Important

AI music can contain artifacts, unexpected lyric pronunciation or similarity to patterns learned by the model. Review outputs before commercial publishing. Do not use voice references you do not have permission to use.
