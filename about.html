ABOUT RIVANI AI

Useful AI tools with a clear job to do.

RIVANI AI is being built as a collection of focused utilities rather than one general-purpose chatbot. Public Beta is centered on three browser-first AI media tools.

RIVANI principlesFocused toolsTransparent statusLUKI assistantExpandable platform

Focused tools first

RIVANI AI currently focuses on three public-Beta AI media tasks: repair a voice recording, enhance an image and remove an image background. Each tool gets its own interface instead of requiring the user to invent prompts.

Status and limits should be visible

Free daily limits, Beta labels, future Pro controls, export choices and realistic failure cases should be visible before they surprise the user. The Advanced Student Calculator is now marked Upcoming rather than being promoted as a live tool.

LUKI is a product guide, not a general chatbot

LUKI answers questions about RIVANI AI tools, account behavior, current Free limits, future plan status, policies and navigation. During Beta it should not quote retired launch pricing or describe the paused QR checkout as active.

Live Beta now, roadmap clearly labeled

The live Beta product is centered on Audio Repair, Image Enhancer and Background Remover. Calculator, Pro and future image/PDF/audio/video/text utilities are labeled Upcoming until they are actually launched.

Smarter tools. Better results.
