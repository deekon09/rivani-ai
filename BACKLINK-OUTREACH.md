# Backlink plan

## Good backlinks to pursue
1. Product directory listings that link directly to rivaniai.online.
2. Genuine reviews/tutorials from creators who actually try one of the three tools.
3. Relevant AI-tool roundup articles.
4. Developer/build-in-public posts that reference the public RIVANI AI site.
5. Useful guest posts about audio cleanup, image enhancement or background removal that naturally cite the relevant RIVANI guide.

## Avoid
- Buying bulk “1,000 backlinks” packages.
- Comment spam.
- Private blog network links.
- Fake reviews.
- Sitewide footer link exchanges with unrelated sites.
- Keyword-stuffed anchor text on every link.

## Preferred anchor text mix
Mostly branded/natural:
- RIVANI AI
- RIVANI AI Audio Repair
- RIVANI AI Image Enhancer
- RIVANI AI Background Remover
- rivaniai.online
- this AI audio repair tool
- this image enhancement guide
