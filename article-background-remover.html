A cutout studio, not only one Remove button

The live tool creates a foreground alpha mask, keeps the source pixels unchanged, and lets you preview the result over transparency, solid colors, blur, a studio gradient or a custom image.

Free accounts get 9 successful removals per day. The quality controls are not reduced on Free; the planned Pro benefit is unlimited daily volume and later high-volume workflow features.

Two browser-side cutout engines

Auto mode prefers a GPU precision engine when WebGPU is stable and automatically falls back to a smaller compatibility engine when needed. The interface does not expose model names; it reports whether GPU precision or compatibility processing completed the job.

Edge and subject repair

  • Portrait / Hair, Product, Glass and Logo/Text alpha presets
  • Edge Clean, Expand/Contract, Feather and Edge Decontaminate
  • Multi-subject region selection when separate foreground regions are detected
  • Manual Erase and Restore brushes with Undo and Reset
  • Cutout Guard edge-confidence score and a Hardest Edge preview
  • Optional generated Studio Shadow and product-canvas padding presets

Exports

  • Transparent PNG master
  • Transparent WebP
  • Composite JPEG with the selected background
  • Alpha Mask PNG
  • Shadow Layer PNG
  • Asset Pack ZIP containing cutout, mask, shadow and preview

Important limits

Hair against a matching background, transparent glass, motion blur, smoke and overlapping subjects can still create uncertain alpha regions. Cutout Guard surfaces this uncertainty instead of claiming every edge is perfect. Use the manual brush when the mask needs a local correction.

Privacy and model download

The image pixels stay in the browser. RIVANI downloads model weights from its model-delivery service, runs inference on the device, and creates exports locally. The first Precision run may take longer because the larger model must be downloaded and cached.
