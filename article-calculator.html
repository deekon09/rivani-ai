What the Advanced Student Calculator does

RIVANI Advanced Student Calculator is built for students who want more than a final number. It combines calculation, explanation and work checking in one focused browser interface. You can prepare an expression, solve supported equations, inspect reasoning, graph functions and use common STEM utilities without switching between separate pages.

Scientific maths and fractions

The scientific workspace supports common arithmetic, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, constants and fractions. Trigonometry uses degrees in the current interface. Use the displayed steps to understand how an answer was formed rather than treating the result as a substitute for learning.

Equations, derivatives and definite integrals

The Solve area includes supported linear and quadratic equations together with derivative and definite-integral workflows. Enter the problem in the format requested by the interface and review the explanation mode that fits your goal: Full Steps, Exam Ready or Easy Hinglish.

Check My Steps

Check My Steps is designed to find the first mismatch in supported equation working. Paste or enter your working line by line. The checker compares the transformation between steps so you can focus on where the reasoning first went wrong instead of only seeing a different final answer.

Graph functions and inspect values

The Graph workspace plots supported functions and can help you inspect roots and values. Graphs are useful for understanding shape and behaviour, but always check the stated domain, units and equation syntax before relying on a visual result.

Probability, statistics, matrices, vectors and units

The calculator also includes probability and statistics tools, unit conversion and dimension checks, matrices, vectors and significant-figure utilities. Everyday tools cover percentages, discounts, geometry, EMI estimates and compound interest.

Important limits

  • Check My Steps supports specific equation formats rather than arbitrary handwritten mathematics.
  • Graphing and calculus inputs must follow the syntax shown by the tool.
  • Financial outputs such as EMI are estimates, not financial advice.
  • For exams and coursework, follow your teacher, syllabus and permitted-calculator rules.
