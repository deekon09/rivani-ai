Use the tools responsibly

Do not use RIVANI AI to violate law, invade privacy, impersonate people deceptively, distribute malware, attack systems or process content you do not have permission to use.

Prohibited technical abuse

  • Attempting to bypass rate limits, authentication or security controls.
  • Automated scraping that materially disrupts the service.
  • Uploading malicious files or using tools to distribute malware.
  • Attempting to access another user’s data or account.

Content and rights

You should respect copyright, privacy, publicity and other rights. Background removal, audio repair and document processing do not create permission to use someone else’s content.

Safety and misuse

Tool-specific restrictions may be added where a feature creates special safety or abuse risks. Those restrictions should be shown on the relevant tool page.

Enforcement

RIVANI AI may block or restrict abusive use to protect users and infrastructure.
