What AI Audio Repair does

RIVANI AI Audio Repair is built for spoken recordings that are understandable but unpleasant to listen to: steady background noise, room tone, hum, fan or road noise, hiss-like noise floors, uneven recording level, difficult phone microphones and other everyday recording problems. The goal is not to turn every damaged recording into a studio master; the goal is to make speech easier and more comfortable to hear while preserving the speaker's natural character.

The main repair path is called RIVANI Clear Voice. The tool keeps the workflow focused: choose or record audio, scan it, set repair strength, process it, compare Before and After, then export.

Current workflow

  1. Choose audio or record from the microphone. The page accepts WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG and FLAC through the upload workflow.
  2. Scan the recording. RIVANI inspects common quality indicators before repair so the interface can show what may need attention.
  3. Set Clear Voice strength. Higher strength removes more unwanted sound but can be less natural on difficult recordings, so the control is deliberately visible.
  4. Run the repair. The selected recording is processed through the RIVANI browser workflow. Keep the tab open while the job is running.
  5. Compare Before and After. Listen at a similar volume and check whether speech remains natural.
  6. Export. Free includes MP3 export; lossless WAV is a Pro feature.
Why comparison matters

Noise reduction is a trade-off. A cleaner waveform is not automatically a better voice. RIVANI exposes Before/After playback so the user can judge clarity, speech detail and naturalness instead of trusting a single score.

What the Free plan includes

  • 9 successful audio repairs per local day
  • Up to 30 minutes and 500 MB per file
  • RIVANI Clear Voice repair
  • Audio scan and repair-strength control
  • Microphone recordings through the same workflow and limits
  • Before/After playback
  • MP3 export

A failed repair does not count as a successful daily job. Processing actions require sign-in.

What Pro is designed to add

Pro is about higher-volume and specialist workflows rather than hiding the basic Clear Voice experience. The current interface describes larger file/processing allowances, unlimited enhancement jobs within the plan allowance, lossless WAV and advanced audio controls.

  • Fan / AC Assist for steady mechanical background sound
  • Traffic Assist for road and engine-band cleanup
  • Click Repair for short impulse-like sounds such as keyboard or mouth clicks
  • Background Voices specialist separation when other voices interfere with the main recording
  • Music Control specialist voice-versus-instrumental workflow
  • De-Reverb dedicated room-echo and late-reflection reduction
  • Lossless WAV export

The website currently recognizes Pro entitlements, but public payment checkout is not connected yet. Any displayed price should be treated as a placeholder until billing goes live.

Good use cases

  • Podcast and interview speech recorded outside a treated studio
  • YouTube, Shorts and Reels voiceovers
  • Lecture and classroom recordings
  • Phone, voice-note and laptop-microphone speech
  • Talking-head creator audio with steady room or fan noise

Important limits

  • Severely clipped speech has information permanently removed and may not be recoverable.
  • Two people talking over each other can remain difficult even with specialist separation.
  • Strong music, heavy reverberation and rapidly changing background sound can leave artifacts.
  • Very aggressive strength can remove parts of breath, consonants or ambience and sound unnatural.
  • The best result is the one that sounds natural to the listener, not necessarily the one with the least background sound.

Privacy and processing

The selected audio is processed in the current RIVANI browser workflow. AI engine files may be downloaded to the device and cached, but the audio-repair page is not designed around uploading the user's recording to a paid RIVANI GPU server. Keep the tab open until repair and export are complete.
